## Local Quick Card Policy

The online local quick card is intentionally limited.

Emergency quick card rules:

- it is local-first only, not a replacement for the cloud controller
- it must stay generic and safe
- it must not claim full SDS grounding
- it should contain only:
  - short incident summary
  - 1-2 `Immediate` actions
  - 1 `Do not` action
  - 1 `Get help` / escalation line

Preventive query rules:

- local model may classify the query first
- the local side must not fabricate rich preventive guidance
- the local side may show only a minimal holding line while cloud checks grounded preventive guidance

Clarification rules:

- if the query is too ambiguous, local asks one short clarification question
- cloud full response should not start until the ambiguity is resolved
